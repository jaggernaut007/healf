# PMID 15846601

Title: Cytidinediphosphocholine (CDP-choline) for cognitive and behavioural disturbances associated with chronic cerebral disorders in the elderly

Summary:
- Cochrane review of randomized, placebo-controlled studies of CDP-choline (citicoline).
- Evidence suggested benefit on memory and behavior outcomes in short to medium-term settings.
- The review reported no clear evidence of attention benefit in the pooled data.
- Citicoline was generally well tolerated in included studies.
- Relevant graph path: Citicoline (CDP-choline) -> Cholinergic membrane support -> Cognitive performance.
