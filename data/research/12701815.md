# PMID 12701815

Title: Effects of creatine supplementation on performance and training adaptations

Summary:
- Comprehensive review of creatine's role as an ergogenic aid for athletes.
- Physiological Impact: Increases total muscle creatine content by 10–30% and phosphocreatine stores by 10–40%.
- Ergogenic Value: ~70% of reviewed studies reported significant improvements in performance.
- Maximal power/strength increased by 5-15%; work performed during sets of maximal-effort muscle contractions improved by 5-15%.
- Creatine supplementation during training programs promotes significantly greater gains in strength and fat-free mass.
- Relevant graph path: Creatine -> ATP production support -> Exercise Performance.
