# PMID 26126245

Title: Tyrosine for Mitigating Stress and Enhancing Performance in Healthy Adult Humans, a Rapid Evidence Assessment of the Literature

Summary:
- Rapid evidence assessment across randomized and controlled studies of supplemental tyrosine.
- Findings did not support a strong recommendation for physical performance under stressful physical conditions.
- A weak recommendation favored tyrosine for cognitive performance under stress.
- The review positions tyrosine as a catecholamine precursor with potential stress-buffering cognitive effects.
- Relevant graph path: L-Tyrosine -> Catecholamine precursor support -> Cognitive stress resilience.
