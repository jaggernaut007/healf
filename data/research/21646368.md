# PMID 21646368

Title: Evaluation, treatment, and prevention of vitamin D deficiency: an Endocrine Society clinical practice guideline

Summary:
- Clinical practice guideline for evaluating, treating, and preventing vitamin D deficiency.
- Measurement of serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D [25(OH)D] is the initial diagnostic test for at-risk patients.
- Recommended treatment with vitamin D2 or vitamin D3 for deficient patients.
- Supplementation recommended at daily intake levels based on age and clinical circumstances.
- Emphasizes the role of Vitamin D in bone health and calcium absorption.
- Relevant graph path: Vitamin D3 -> Calcium absorption support -> Bone Health.
