# PMID 23439798

Title: A prospective, randomized double-blind, placebo-controlled study of safety and efficacy of a high-concentration full-spectrum extract of ashwagandha root in reducing stress and anxiety in adults

Summary:
- Ashwagandha reduced stress and anxiety scores in stressed adults.
- Serum cortisol decreased in the treatment group versus placebo.
- Relevant graph path: Ashwagandha -> Cortisol reduction -> Stress.
