# PMID 20079786

Title: L-theanine and caffeine improve task switching but not intersensory attention or subjective alertness

Summary:
- Randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind, cross-over trial evaluating acute L-theanine + caffeine.
- Dose pair in this study: L-theanine 97 mg and caffeine 40 mg.
- The combination significantly improved attention on a task-switching test versus placebo.
- Subjective alertness and intersensory attention were not significantly improved.
- Relevant graph path: L-Theanine/Caffeine -> Selective attention support -> Focus.
