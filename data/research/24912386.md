# PMID 24912386

Title: The International Scientific Association for Probiotics and Prebiotics consensus statement on the scope and appropriate use of the term probiotic

Summary:
- Consensus statement on the definition and scope of probiotics.
- Definition: "live microorganisms which when administered in adequate amounts confer a health benefit on the host."
- Requirements: Live microorganisms, adequate dosage, and demonstrated health benefit.
- Emphasizes the importance of strain-specific benefits and clinical evidence.
- Relevant graph path: Probiotics -> Microbiome diversity support -> Gut Health.
