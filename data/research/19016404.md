# PMID 19016404

Title: A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study of the standardised extract SHR-5 of Rhodiola rosea in stress-related fatigue

Summary:
- Phase III randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in subjects with stress-related fatigue.
- Rhodiola rosea SHR-5 showed anti-fatigue effects and improved concentration-related attention metrics versus placebo.
- Cortisol response to awakening stress was improved in the treatment group.
- No serious side effects attributable to the extract were reported.
- Relevant graph path: Rhodiola rosea -> Stress-adaptation and fatigue modulation -> Stress-related fatigue.
