Title: Use of condensed tannin extract from quebracho trees to reduce methane emissions from cattle

Summary:
- Quebracho tannin extract was tested as a methane-mitigation feed additive in cattle.
- The study did not show a methane reduction at the tested levels, but it remains the source literature for the quebracho corpus item.
