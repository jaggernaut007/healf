# PMID 23914213

Title: Zinc requirements and the risks and benefits of zinc supplementation

Summary:
- Overview of zinc nutrition, human requirements, and supplementation benefits vs. toxicity risks.
- Physiological Importance: 2–3 grams in the human body; ~0.1% replenished daily.
- Deficiency Risks: ~25% of the world's population is at risk.
- Toxicity: Excessive zinc can induce copper deficiency due to their interaction.
- Narrow safety margin between RDA and toxic levels.
- Crucial for immune function and various metabolic processes.
- Relevant graph path: Zinc -> Immune enzyme support -> Immunity.
