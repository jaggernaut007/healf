# 41817298

## Study Details
**Study Type:** Randomized Controlled Trial
**Sample Size:** 120 participants
**Dosage Tested:** 500mg Agastache rugosa extract complex / day

## Abstract
This study evaluates the effects of Agastache rugosa extract and a complex of Agastache rugosa, Ficus carica, and Lycium barbarum extracts on skin aging. Participants were given either the complex or a placebo. Results showed significant improvements in skin elasticity, moisture retention, and reduction in wrinkle depth compared to the placebo group. The supplement promotes collagen synthesis and reduces oxidative stress in the skin.

## Key Finding
Supplementation with the extract complex significantly improved skin hydration and reduced signs of aging, likely due to enhanced collagen production.
