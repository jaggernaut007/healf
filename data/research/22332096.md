# PMID 22332096

Title: Omega-3 fatty acids EPA and DHA: health benefits throughout life

Summary:
- Review of EPA and DHA (long-chain omega-3 PUFAs) roles across the lifespan.
- Essential dietary necessity; obtained through fish or supplements.
- Critical for fetal development: neuronal, retinal, and immune system function.
- Linked to cardiovascular function (inflammation, anticoagulation) and cognitive function.
- Incorporated into cell membranes; plays essential roles in anti-inflammatory processes.
- Relevant graph path: Omega-3 -> Anti-inflammatory signaling support -> Heart Health.
