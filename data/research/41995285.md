# 41995285

## Study Details
**Study Type:** Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
**Sample Size:** 450 participants
**Dosage Tested:** 300-500mg Magnesium / day

## Abstract
Magnesium is commonly used to treat sleep disorders. This meta-analysis reviewed 10 randomized controlled trials evaluating the effect of magnesium supplementation on sleep quality, particularly in older adults with insomnia. Results indicate that magnesium supplementation improves subjective measures of insomnia such as ISI score, sleep efficiency, sleep duration and sleep onset latency.

## Key Finding
Magnesium supplementation significantly improves sleep quality and reduces insomnia severity, especially in older adults.
