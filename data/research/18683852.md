# PMID 18683852

Title: A randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind trial of Bacopa monnieri in healthy older persons

Summary:
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of standardized Bacopa monnieri extract in healthy older adults.
- Bacopa improved delayed verbal recall and showed improvement on Stroop-related performance versus placebo.
- Anxiety and depression scale trends also improved in the Bacopa group during the intervention.
- Tolerability was generally acceptable, with mild gastrointestinal complaints reported.
- Relevant graph path: Bacopa monnieri -> Memory and attentional control support -> Brain fog.
